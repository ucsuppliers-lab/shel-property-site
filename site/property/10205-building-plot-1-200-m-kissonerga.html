Before you buy this plot

  • Ask an architect to confirm what you can build: the zone, building density, coverage and height all limit the size of the house.
  • Your lawyer checks the title deed, any mortgage on the land, and that the access road is a registered public road.
  • A private owner selling occasionally doesn't usually charge VAT, so you pay transfer fees instead. Confirm it in the contract.
  • Buying from outside the EU? Permits are usually for one home with a limited plot, and the rules under discussion would ban farmland and cap plot sizes. Speak to a lawyer before you offer.
