Accessibility

We aim to meet the WCAG 2.2 AA standard: pages that work with a keyboard and a screen reader, text that can be enlarged, and colours with enough contrast. The Hebrew pages read right to left.

If something doesn't work for you, tell us and we'll reply within two working days.

Draft. We'll test the live site with disabled users before launch and publish what we find here.
