Finance and mortgages

Most buyers from abroad pay in pounds or shekels and the seller wants euros. How you move the money changes what the home really costs you.

Moving money

Banks and specialist currency firms offer different rates and fees. Compare the total you'll pay, not just the headline rate, and agree with your lawyer how deposits are held.

Mortgages

Some buyers borrow against a home they already own; others look for a mortgage in Cyprus. Which suits you depends on your income, where you live and the property.

Introductions

We can introduce you to regulated currency firms and mortgage brokers. We don't give financial advice, and you deal with them directly.
