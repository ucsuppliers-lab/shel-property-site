For agents

For licensed agents and developers

Buyers from three countries, ready to talk

Shel Property brings buyers from the UK, Germany and Israel to your Paphos listings. Each enquiry reaches you with the buyer's budget, timing and how they'll pay. The first 30 offices to connect their listing feed pay nothing for three months.

What you get

Enquiries with a brief

Name and contact, plus budget, when they want to buy, how they'll pay, whether they're EU or non-EU, and how they want to view. You can tell a serious buyer at a glance.

Three languages

Your listings shown in English, German and Hebrew, with a buyer desk that answers buyers in their own language.

A report every month

Every enquiry with the buyer's country and budget, whether you replied and how fast, viewings booked, and views and saves on each listing. See a sample.

What we ask of you

  1. Your licenceWe check your registration and licence with the Registration Council before you go live, and again each January.
  2. Your listing feedFrom the system you already use, such as Qobrix or Estatebud, so prices and sales update on their own. A failed import never takes your listings down.
  3. The required factsEnergy class, VAT treatment and title deed status on every sale listing, before it goes live.
  4. Permission to publishWritten agreement that we can show your photos, text and plans, and translate them.
  5. RepliesWe ask buyers after 48 hours whether you answered. Your reply times are private to you unless you choose to show a "fast reply" badge.
