Legal checks and title deeds

Before you pay anything or sign a contract in Cyprus, have your own lawyer, one who works for you and not for the seller or the agent.

What your lawyer checks

  • Who owns the property, and whether a title deed has been issued.
  • Whether there's a mortgage or charge on the property or the land it stands on.
  • That the building has its planning and building permits.
  • The contract, and that it's deposited at the Land Registry.

Our guide to title deeds explains the different deed situations you'll see on listings.
