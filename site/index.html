On every home for sale

A sale listing can't go live on Shel until the first four are filled in.

How we check
  • The agent's licenceRegistration and licence numbers, plus the date we checked them and where.
  • Energy classCyprus law requires it in every sale or rental advert for a building with a certificate.
  • VAT treatmentResale with no VAT, or a new build with VAT on top, and when the 5% rate might apply.
  • Title deed statusIssued, after completion or pending, as the agent declares it. Your lawyer confirms it.
  • What it costs to buyTransfer fees, VAT and legal fees worked out on the price, with our sources.
  • Listed and last confirmedTwo dates, so you can see the home is still on the market.
