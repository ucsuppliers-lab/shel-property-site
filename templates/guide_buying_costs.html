On top of the price, a buyer in Cyprus pays Land Registry transfer fees on a resale, or VAT on a new build, and their lawyer. On a €250,000 resale the transfer fees come to €6,600. Work it out on any price.

Land Registry transfer fees

Charged on resales, in bands, and currently reduced by half:

Part of the priceRate before the reduction
Up to €85,0003%
€85,001 to €170,0005%
Over €170,0008%

On a €250,000 resale that works out at €6,600 after the 50% reduction. If the Land Registry thinks the price is below the property's value, it can charge on its own valuation instead. New builds bought with VAT don't normally pay transfer fees.

Stamp duty

Still to confirm. EY reports that Cyprus abolished stamp duty on contracts from 1 January 2026, but some guides published since still list it at 0.15% from €5,001 to €170,000 and 0.2% above. On €250,000 that would be €408. A Cyprus tax adviser is checking which is right.

Your lawyer

Legal fees usually run at 1% to 2% of the price. That should cover the Land Registry searches, the contract, stamping and lodging it, and the transfer.

Sources: Your Overseas Home, Cyprus property buying costs, updated 27 May 2026; EY, Cyprus tax reform 2026; lawyer.com.cy, taxes and fees when buying property in Cyprus.
