About Shel Property

Shel Property is a property portal for Cyprus, starting in Paphos. Licensed agents and developers list resale homes, new developments, land and homes to rent. Buyers and tenants search for free.

We started with the questions buyers in Cyprus ask first. So every listing shows whether the title deed is issued, whether VAT is added, how far it is to the sea, and the agent's registration and licence numbers.

We're a UK company. We publish adverts from agents and developers; we aren't an estate agent ourselves, and we don't take part in any sale or letting.
