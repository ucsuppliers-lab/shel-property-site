Property for sale in Polis

A small town on Chrysochou Bay, forty minutes north, near the Akamas. Homes from licensed agents, with the title deed status shown on every listing.

Before you buy

Resale homes carry no VAT and pay Land Registry transfer fees. New builds, and land sold by a business, carry VAT instead. Whatever you buy, ask whether the title deed is issued, and buying from outside the EU means a permit first.
