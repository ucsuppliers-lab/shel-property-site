Buyer desk

Ask us first. We'll make sure the agent answers.

We reply in English, German or Hebrew within 15 minutes, 09:00 to 19:00 Cyprus time, seven days a week. Outside those hours, we answer first thing the next morning.

What we do for you

  1. Answer your questionsAbout an area, a listing, the permit, VAT or costs. If it needs a lawyer, we say so and tell you why.
  2. Pass your enquiry onTo the agent for the home you asked about, with your budget, timing and how you'll pay, so they can answer properly the first time.
  3. Check you got a reply48 hours later we ask you. If the agent hasn't answered, we chase them, and we note it in the agent's monthly report.
  4. Pass on viewing requestsFor a live video walk-through, or for a trip where you see homes from several agents. You choose the homes; each agent books its own viewing with you.

We don't negotiate for you or recommend one agent over another, and nobody pays us when a home sells. Your own lawyer acts for you in the purchase.

Ask the buyer desk

We use your details to answer you, and pass them to an agent only if you ask us to. Privacy notice.

Plan a viewing trip

Save the homes you want to see, then tell us when you're flying. We send your dates to each agent, and each one books its own viewing with you. We put the confirmed times into one plan, area by area, so you're not crossing the district twice in a day.

Paphos airport has flights from the UK all year, and in summer 2026 there were direct flights from Berlin, Cologne, Düsseldorf, Memmingen and Munich. Many flights from Israel land at Larnaca, about 90 minutes' drive away.
