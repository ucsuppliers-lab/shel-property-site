Residency

Owning a home in Cyprus and living there aren't the same thing. Buyers from the UK and Israel are non-EU buyers, so the rules on staying longer than a holiday are different from those for EU citizens.

Residency rules change, and the right route depends on your nationality, income and plans. That's why we don't give immigration advice ourselves.
