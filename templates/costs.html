How we work it out

  • Transfer fees on resales: 3% of the first €85,000, 5% from €85,001 to €170,000, and 8% above that, then halved. New builds bought with VAT don't normally pay them.
  • VAT on new builds: 19%. The 5% rate can apply to the first 130 m² and the first €350,000 if it will be your main home, but not at all if the home is over 190 m² or the price is over €475,000. The Tax Department has to approve it, and you repay the difference if you move out within 10 years.
  • Land: since January 2018, building land sold by a business carries 19% VAT, with no transfer fees. A private owner selling occasionally usually doesn't charge VAT, so transfer fees apply. Agricultural and protected land is outside the VAT rule. The 5% rate is only for homes.
  • Legal fees usually run at 1% to 2% of the price. We use 1% plus VAT.

Stamp duty, still to confirm. EY reports that Cyprus abolished stamp duty on contracts from 1 January 2026. Some buyers' guides published since still list it at 0.15% and 0.2%. We leave it out unless you tick the box, until a Cyprus tax adviser confirms which is right.

Sources: Your Overseas Home, Cyprus property buying costs, May 2026; ETEK, VAT amending law guidance; EY, Cyprus tax reform 2026; PwC, Cyprus other taxes, EY and Mondaq on VAT on land. Checked 23 October 2026.
