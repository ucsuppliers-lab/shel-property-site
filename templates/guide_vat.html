When a price on Shel Property says "+ VAT", VAT is added on top. That's almost always a new build bought from a developer. Resales of homes that have been lived in don't carry VAT.

The standard rate

New homes and building land are charged at the standard VAT rate of 19%.

The reduced 5% rate

If the home will be your main and permanent residence, you may pay 5% on part of it instead. Under the rules as amended in 2023 and 2024:

  • The 5% rate covers the first 130 m² of the home.
  • It covers the first €350,000 of the price.
  • Homes over 190 m², or costing more than €475,000, get no 5% rate at all.
  • If you stop living there within ten years, you repay some or all of the difference.
  • The Tax Department has to approve it. A holiday home doesn't qualify.

On a listing we show the property's VAT treatment: resale with no VAT, or a new build with VAT on top. Whether you get the 5% rate depends on you, so we never show it as the price.

The figures depend on your circumstances and on how the tax office reads the law, so ask your lawyer to confirm before you sign.

Transfer fees and VAT

Land Registry transfer fees are charged on resales. If you pay VAT on a new build, you don't normally pay transfer fees as well. See the costs of buying.

Sources: G. Vrikis & Associates on Mondaq, "Changes to immovable property reduced VAT rate of 5%", 6 September 2024; ETEK, VAT amending law guidance; lawyer.com.cy, taxes and fees when buying property in Cyprus.
