Before you buy off plan

  • Your lawyer checks the developer's land at the Land Registry, including any mortgage on it, and asks how the mortgage will be lifted from your home.
  • Tie each stage payment to a stage of the building work in the contract, and have your lawyer lodge the contract at the Land Registry.
  • Ask when the separate title deed is due, and what happens if the finish date slips.
  • Buying from outside the EU? You'll need a permit, and residency through a new home has its own conditions. Do I need a permit?
